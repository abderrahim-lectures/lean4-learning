## Unreleased — Derivation-first style pass, whole book

Reverses part of the v1.5.0/fifth-pass narrative direction (see
[README.md](../README.md#how-to-read-this-book)): chapter openers and
bare "Picture it like this" analogy boxes are replaced with exposition
that derives each definition and theorem from the question that forces
it, rather than stating it and explaining afterward, in the tradition of
Arnold's and Gelfand's teaching. No fixed Definition→Theorem→Proof
template is imposed. Chapter 7 was the pilot; every other chapter (0–6,
8–14) now has the same treatment.

### Changes

- **Every chapter's `00-index.md`.** Removed the "## The story of this
  chapter" narrative-opener section (a numbered list of rhetorical
  questions) and replaced it with continuous derivation-first prose,
  preserving every fact, citation, and cross-reference target.
- **Every chapter's exercises file.** Removed "Socratic questions" blocks
  that stated a question and answered it inline. Where the content was a
  genuine problem, it is now a plain, unanswered exercise, and its
  solution was added to the matching file in
  [15-appendix-solutions/](../15-appendix-solutions/00-index.md). Where
  the content was reflective recap rather than a provable problem (parts
  of Chapters 13–14), it was folded into the surrounding "Key points"
  prose instead.
- **`lean_book/07-groups/`, all sections.** Additionally rewrote the
  chapter body prose (not just the index/exercises), as the pilot for
  this pass — see the section-by-section rationale in the earlier
  "Chapter 7 pilot" entry, folded into this release.
- **`09-rings/01-definition.md`.** Rewrote the ring-axiom statement to
  derive the axioms (why addition strengthens to commutative, why
  multiplication needs no inverses, why distributivity is the necessary
  link) rather than stating R1–R4 cold.
- **New Lean lemmas** `nat_add_zero` and `nat_zero_mul`, added to
  `lean_book/15-appendix-solutions/04-chapter-4.md` and
  `05-chapter-5.md` as solutions to two newly stated exercises, mirrored
  in `lean_project/LeanProject/Ch15AppendixSolutions.lean`.
- **`CONTRIBUTING.md` and `README.md`.** Documented the derivation-first
  prose convention as the book's style throughout, no longer scoped to a
  single pilot chapter.
- **`05-tactics/02-core-tactics.md`.** A follow-up audit found this file
  had not actually been converted, still a flat tactic-by-tactic catalog
  (name the tactic, show code). Rewritten to derive `intro`/`exact`/
  `apply`/`rw`/`rw ... at h` from the term-mode proof of `modus_ponens`,
  each tactic motivated by a specific limitation of the ones before it.
- **`01-basics/00-index.md`, `03-functions-and-structures/00-index.md`.**
  The same audit found a numbered Q&A scaffold (Chapter 1) and a
  reassuring aside ("this is not needed to follow along," Chapter 3)
  inconsistent with the rest of the book's terser register. Both fixed.
- **Three citation gaps closed**, found by a full-book citation audit:
  `04-propositions-and-proofs/04-implication.md` now cross-references
  Section 1's Curry–Howard citation; `13-working-efficiently/03-simp.md`
  now cites [Huet1980] for the confluence claim (already used for the
  same fact in Chapter 2); `14-next-steps/02-moving-to-mathlib.md` now
  cites [DummitFoote2003] §3.2 for Lagrange's theorem, previously
  invoked by name with no citation.

Body prose in most chapters (2–6, 8–14) was already close to
derivation-first in style before this pass — each rewrite was scoped to
the two recurring violations above (narrative openers, answered-inline
exercises) rather than a wholesale rewrite, to avoid introducing errors
into text that already satisfied the rule.

### Verification

- `lake build` in `lean_project/` passes, 8681 jobs, zero errors. The
  only fenced-code diffs anywhere in the book are the two new lemmas
  above (pure additions, not modifications to existing code).
- Every exercise/appendix-solution pair checked in sync by file and
  number.
- Cross-references spot-checked against real target headings, including
  Chapter 7's "Chapter 9, Section 7" reference to the matrix
  non-commutativity example, and Chapter 8's Theorem 1/2/3 references.
- A full-book follow-up audit (citation completeness, leftover style
  violations, index-page register consistency, chapter-transition
  coherence, exercise-style consistency) found the bibliography itself
  fully in sync (26 keys, no orphans either direction) and no leftover
  narrative/analogy/answered-inline-exercise violations, but did find
  the four gaps listed above. All four are fixed in this release.
- `lake build` re-run after the follow-up fixes: still 8681 jobs, zero
  errors; `python3 lean_book_latex/build/build_latex.py` regenerates
  cleanly (114 section files).

This bumps the major version, not a patch, because the change is a
reversal of the book's stated prose philosophy (see
[README.md](../README.md#how-to-read-this-book)) across every chapter,
not an incremental fix.
