## v2.0.6 — Rewrite "About this book" and add a "Pedagogical approach" chapter

### What changed

- **"About this book" rewritten.** The chapter now states the book's
  scope and prerequisites directly (a from-scratch introduction to
  Lean~4 for mathematicians, no programming background assumed) instead
  of opening with the AI-authorship note.
- **New "Pedagogical approach" chapter.** States the book's teaching
  method explicitly: derivation over dispensation, in the tradition of
  Arnold and Gelfand rather than Bourbaki, with no fixed
  Definition-Theorem-Proof-Remark skeleton imposed on the exposition.
- **New "A note on authorship" chapter.** The AI-authorship disclosure
  previously folded into "About this book" now has its own chapter,
  kept and rewritten as a concise, professional summary (the earlier
  version's blow-by-blow narration of specific defects found in past
  review rounds was cut in favor of a pointer to `reviews/` and the
  CHANGELOG, where that detail already lives).
- **Prose-style pass on the new front matter.** All three chapters were
  swept against this book's own style rules (no possessives, no
  colon-led explanations) before landing.

### Release changes

- **Version bump.** `\bookversion` in `lean_book_latex/preamble.tex`
  advanced to `v2.0.6`.
- **PDF rebuilt.** The release PDF was regenerated through the LaTeX
  pipeline and attached to this release.
- **README updated.** PDF download links point at the `v2.0.6` asset
  name.
