## v2.0.1 — Regenerate stale LaTeX, close the review backlog

`v2.0.0` shipped with ten `lean_book_latex/` files silently out of sync
with their Markdown source — every fix made after the release's earlier
follow-up passes (root README rewrite, `Mat2.ext`/matrices fix, currying
derivation, quiver definition, notation-reference correction, `neg_mul`
appendix attribution) was never regenerated into `.tex`, in violation of
`CONTRIBUTING.md`'s "the LaTeX/PDF pipeline regenerates `lean_book_latex/`
from Markdown automatically" rule.

### Fixes

- **Stale LaTeX tree.** Ran `build_pdf.sh` end to end
  (`build_latex.py` + 3x `xelatex` + `biber`) to regenerate
  `lean_book_latex/` from current Markdown. Ten files changed:
  `03-functions-and-structures/00-index.tex`,
  `04-propositions-and-proofs/03-theorem-lemma.tex`,
  `05-tactics/04-more-tactics.tex`, `08-group-theorems/05-exercises.tex`,
  `09-rings/07-matrices.tex`, `10-ring-theorems/04-exercises.tex`,
  `12-path-algebras/01-what-is-a-quiver.tex`,
  `12-path-algebras/07-checkpoint-project.tex`,
  `13-working-efficiently/05-structuring-lemmas.tex`,
  `notation-reference.tex`.
- **`04-propositions-and-proofs/03-theorem-lemma.md`.** `theorem`/`lemma`
  was stated cold; added the forcing-question lead-in a `reviews/2026-08-15`
  finding suggested (why `def` alone cannot assert `2+2=4` is *true*).
- **`08-group-theorems/05-exercises.md`, `10-ring-theorems/04-exercises.md`.**
  Two exercises gave away their full tactic-by-tactic strategy inline, in
  tension with `CONTRIBUTING.md`'s "should not give away their own
  answer" rule; trimmed to one-clause pointers, verified the full
  derivations still live in the matching appendix solutions.
- **`CONTRIBUTING.md`.** Softened the "no fixed skeleton" claim to
  explicitly exempt Chapters 8/10's deliberate six-box theorem template.
- **`12-path-algebras/07-checkpoint-project.md`.** "proves it behaves
  the way it obviously should under composition" replaced with the
  actual theorem statement,
  `(Path.append p q).length = p.length + q.length`.
- **27 dead relative links** in `changelog/v1.1.0.md`, `v1.2.0.md`,
  `v1.4.0.md`, left over from a chapter-2 insertion earlier in the
  book's history that shifted chapters 2-13 and appendix solutions
  3-11 up by one. Remapped every link using the actual git rename
  history, then re-verified with a full link-existence sweep across
  `lean_book/` (zero broken relative links, zero broken images remain).

### Verification

- `lake build` in `lean_project/`: 8681 jobs, zero errors; no
  `sorry`/`admit`/`axiom`/`unsafe` anywhere.
- `lean_audit.py`: zero mismatches between Markdown Lean blocks and the
  compiled project.
- Full PDF rebuild: 302 pages, zero undefined references, zero
  undefined citations. Remaining warnings (2x oversized float, 1x
  hyperref bookmark anchor, 1x font-shape substitution) are the same
  pre-existing, out-of-scope issues documented in `v2.0.0.md`.
- A structural sweep (broken links, broken images, unbalanced
  `**`/code fences, self-referencing navigation strips, duplicate-word
  artifacts) and a style-specific pass against `CONTRIBUTING.md`'s
  "Book prose conventions" found nothing further to fix.
- `proof-search-narrative-reviewer` and `lean-code-book-auditor`'s
  trace-correctness check (both ported to `~/dev/research-ideas` from
  this repo, applied here against their source): every narrated tactic
  failure in Chapter 10 (`rw [h1]` occurrence targeting, `conv_lhs`
  unavailability) reproduced verbatim against the pinned toolchain;
  every `dbg_trace` narration (Chapter 1's `Vec.replicate'`/`Vec.dot'`,
  Chapter 12's `Path.append'`/`Path.length'`) matches actual runtime
  output exactly.

This is a patch release, not a major one: every change here closes a
gap left by `v2.0.0`'s own passes (a sync bug and the last LOW-severity
review findings), it does not change the book's prose philosophy.
