## v1.6.2 — Bibliography citations for Chapter 4, Ring, Module, and LinearMap

Closes citation gaps found by a two-pass review (a broad book-wide sweep,
then an exhaustive file-by-file re-check) of which formal notions in the
book state a claim without a source.

### Additions

- **Chapter 4 (Propositions and proofs).** Added "Sources, quoted" boxes
  to `05-and-or-not.md` (And/Or/Not as product/coproduct/initial object,
  citing a newly added reference, [Agore2023]), `06-quantifiers.md`
  (universal/existential quantifiers as Π/Σ-types, citing [TPIL4] and
  cross-referencing the existing [HoTT2013] citation), and
  `07-equality.md` (equality as an equivalence relation and the
  substitution/congruence property, citing [TPIL4]).
- **Ring, Module, and LinearMap definitions.** The primary definitions in
  `09-rings/01-definition.md`, `11-modules/01-definition.md`, and
  `11-modules/05-linear-maps.md` had no citation at all, unlike the
  parallel `Group` (Chapter 7) and `Quiver` (Chapter 12) definitions.
  All three now cite [DummitFoote2003] with section references.
- **New bibliography entry.** [Agore2023], *A First Course in Category
  Theory* (Springer, 2023), for the product/coproduct/initial-object
  citations above.

### Fixes

- **Two pre-existing broken LaTeX citations.** [Coquand2021] and
  [Huet1980] were cited in the book text and present in
  `lean_book/bibliography.md`, but missing from the LaTeX-side
  `lean_book_latex/references.bib`, so the PDF build silently rendered
  them as undefined references. Both `.bib` entries are now added,
  alongside the new [Agore2023] entry.
