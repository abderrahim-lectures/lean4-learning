## v1.4.25 — Toolchain bump to Lean v4.32.2; Bloom verbs made implicit

**Toolchain update** (aligned with PR #239, `opencode/update/lean-v4.32.2`):

- `lean_project/lean-toolchain`: bumped from `leanprover/lean4:v4.31.0` to
  `leanprover/lean4:v4.32.2`.
- `lean_project/lakefile.toml`: Mathlib `rev` bumped from `v4.31.0` to
  `v4.32.2`; all dependency git revisions updated to match the v4.32.2
  tag.
- `lean_project/lake-manifest.json`: all pinned dependencies (mathlib,
  plausible, importGraph, proofwidgets, aesop, Qq, batteries, Cli)
  regenerated via `lake update` against the v4.32.2 toolchain.
- Version references updated across `README.md`, `NOTICE.md`,
  `lean_book/README.md`, `lean_project/README.md`,
  `lean_book/00-setup/02-installing-toolchain.md`,
  `lean_book/learning-paths.md`, and
  `lean_book/00-setup/04-mathlib-note.md`.

**Bloom verbs made implicit:** Removed the explicit "**Learning objectives.**"
paragraph from every chapter's `00-index.md` (Chapters 0–13). The cognitive
progression those objectives stated is now woven into the chapter's narrative
intro: chapters that already had a "The story of this chapter" section now
lead directly into it, and chapters that lacked one (00, 04, 12, 13) gained a
new story section that implicitly guides the reader through the same
expectations. No section content, Lean code, exercises, or "Key points"
recaps were altered — only the explicit objectives listing was removed and
the narrative framing adjusted.
