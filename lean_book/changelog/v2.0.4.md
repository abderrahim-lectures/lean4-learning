## v2.0.4 — New proof strategy guide

This release adds a new reference page to the book, the
[proof strategy guide](../proof-strategy-guide.md), cross-referenced from
Chapter 5 and Chapter 13.

### New

- **Proof strategy guide (`proof-strategy-guide.md`).** A new reference
  page that maps the *shape* of the goal, hypothesis, definition, or
  algebraic structure you are currently facing to the tactic most likely
  to make progress first, for that specific case. It is the reverse of
  the existing tactic and library reference (which indexes tactics by
  name) and of the notation and λ-calculus dictionaries (which translate
  symbols). Rows are keyed to the exercise and theorem patterns of the
  book itself: the `n + 0 = n` versus `0 + n = n` split (which `Nat.add`
  clause is `rfl` and which needs induction, because `Nat.add` recurses
  on the second argument), the "regroup, then cancel" inverse pattern of
  Chapter 8, Theorem 3, the `congr 1` direct-sum split of Chapter 11, and
  the `Path.append` induction of Chapter 12. Every tactic listed is one
  the book itself uses and teaches; `noncomm_ring` is flagged in its row
  as Mathlib-only.
- **Cross-references.** The page is linked from the book
  [reference index](README.md), from the Chapter 5 index (where a reader
  first meets tactic mode), and from the Chapter 13 index (where the
  efficient-use tactics are introduced).

### Verification

- **Faithfulness.** Every axiom name, field name, and structure name the
  guide references was checked against the companion Lean project and the
  definitions of the book itself: `Grp.inv_right`, `Grp.inv_left`, `Grp.assoc`,
  `Grp.id_right`; `Module.smul_add`/`one_smul`, `LinearMap.map_add`/
  `map_smul`, `Submodule.carrier`/closure fields, `Path.append`/
  `Path.length`, and `neg_one_mul`/`mul_zero` all exist verbatim
  where cited. Mathlib-only names the guide mentions (`noncomm_ring`,
  `mul_add`, `add_comm`) are explicitly scoped as Mathlib names in their
  rows, not presented as fields of the own structures of the book.
- **Tactic vocabulary.** Every tactic named in the guide appears in the
  tactic and library reference of the book (its official per-tactic
  index); no tactic is invented for the page.
- **Lean integrity.** `lake build` in `lean_project/` remains clean; no
  Lean code or math changed. The change set is `proof-strategy-guide.md`
  (new), four prose cross-references (README, Ch. 5 index, Ch. 13 index,
  changelog index), the changelog entry, and the `\bookversion` bump in
  `preamble.tex`.

This is a patch release: it adds one lookup page and its links without
changing the derivation-first prose philosophy of the book or any
mathematics.
