## v1.5.3 — Book-wide prose style pass: no colons, em-dashes, or possessives

A full editorial pass over every chapter (1–14), the appendix solutions,
and the front-matter reference pages (`README.md`, `lean_book/README.md`,
`notation-reference.md`, `tactic-and-library-reference.md`,
`lambda-calculus-dictionary.md`, `learning-paths.md`, `bibliography.md`),
rewriting prose to avoid colons and em-dashes, which read as ambiguous
against Lean's own `:` (type ascription) and `--` (line comment) syntax,
and English possessive ('s) constructions. Code blocks, LaTeX math,
chapter/section titles, and direct quotes from external sources are
untouched. `lean_book/changelog/` was left out of scope, since it is
release history rather than reader-facing narrative prose.

### Notes

- Every fix landed in the Markdown source; `lean_book_latex/` was
  regenerated from it via `build_latex.py`.
- Includes a self-audit (`reviews/2026-08-07/REVIEW.md`) that caught and
  fixed two bugs introduced during earlier editorial passes: a
  forgetful-functor diagram/table mismatch in Chapter 1, and a
  notation-reference label collision.
