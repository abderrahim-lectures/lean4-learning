## v2.0.5 — Release the derivation-first book

This is the first release to bring the derivation-first rewrite
(developed on the `derivation-first-rewrite` branch and merged through
[pull request #261](https://github.com/abderrahim-lectures/lean4-learning/pull/261))
onto `master`. It consolidates the v2.0.0-v2.0.4 work, namely the
whole-book concrete-first prose pass, the proof strategy guide, the
prose-rule closeout, and the exhaustive deep review, and ships it with a
version bump, a rebuilt PDF, and a re-synced online site.

### What lands on `master`

- **The derivation-first rewrite (v2.0.0).** Chapter openers and bare
  analogy boxes are replaced with exposition that derives each definition
  and theorem from the question that forces it, in the tradition of
  Arnold and Gelfand. Every chapter 0-14 received the treatment; see
  [v2.0.0](v2.0.0.md) for the details.
- **The proof strategy guide.** The new
  [proof strategy guide](../proof-strategy-guide.md) maps goal, hypothesis,
  definition, and algebraic-structure shapes to the tactic to try first,
  verified against the book and the companion Lean project. See
  [v2.0.4](v2.0.4.md).
- **Prose-rule and review closeout (v2.0.1-v2.0.3).** Regenerated stale
  LaTeX, closed the review backlog, finished the prose-style sweep
  (no possessives, no em-dashes), and completed the exhaustive deep
  review of every chapter. See [v2.0.1](v2.0.1.md),
  [v2.0.2](v2.0.2.md), [v2.0.3](v2.0.3.md).
- **Update from "Russian-style" to "derivation-first".** The working name
  of the rewrite and its branch are, from here on, derivation-first
  throughout.

### Release changes

- **Version bump.** `\bookversion` in `lean_book_latex/preamble.tex`
  advanced to `v2.0.5`.
- **Lean toolchain bump.** The companion project and the book now target
  Lean toolchain `leanprover/lean4:v4.33.1` (Mathlib pinned to the same
  tag), up from `v4.32.2`, across `lean-toolchain`, `lakefile.toml`, and
  every live reference in the READMEs, NOTICE, REPRODUCING, the setup
  chapters, and the learning-paths pages.
- **PDF rebuilt.** The release PDF was regenerated from the derivation-first
  Markdown through the LaTeX pipeline and attached to this release.
- **Online site re-synced.** The `gh-pages` site (a flattened Jekyll
  mirror of `lean_book/`) was brought in line with the merged book
  content, including the new proof strategy guide.
- **READMEs updated.** The book README, the reference index, and the
  changelog index reflect the current state of the project.
- **Learning paths and graph updated.** The stale LaTeX chapter
  dependency diagram was corrected to the current 0-14 chapter structure
  (it had dropped the Terminology & CoC chapter and used the old
  numbering), matching the `learning-paths.md` Mermaid graph.

This is a release of accumulated v2.x work plus the deployment tooling;
no new mathematics was added beyond what the v2.0.x cuts documented.
